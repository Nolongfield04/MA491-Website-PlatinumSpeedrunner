Self-Hosted Weather Station & Landing Recreation Planner

A weather site running on a Raspberry Pi at home (Flask, cron, NOAA + Open-Meteo APIs). Tracks 5 cities (West Point NY, Houston TX, Lampasas TX, Paris FR, Stowe VT) hourly, with a past-month history view per city and a page to look up current conditions for any other city in the world on demand. Also includes a "D-Day landing recreation" planner that scores upcoming dates for an amphibious/airborne landing recreation by wave height and moon phase — selectable across coastal military sites in the US, Latin America, and Europe. Wave forecasts beyond the marine model's ~9-day horizon are estimated from real prior-year readings the site logs every day, building its own climatology over time.

Golf Tee Time Advisor

Search a golf course (or its nearest town), and it scores every plausible tee time over the next 7 days — daylight hours where an 18-hole round could finish before sunset — on rain chance, wind speed, and temperature comfort. Thunderstorm risk or wind gusts at/over 30mph are a hard safety cutoff, not just a scoring penalty. Runs on the same Raspberry Pi as the weather station, fetching live from Open-Meteo per search rather than a fixed course list.

Fantasy Football Lineup Optimizer

Connects to my ESPN Fantasy Football league via the ESPN Fantasy API to pull my roster, free agents, and injury statuses fresh on every page load. Combines that with real historical defense-vs-position performance data (nflverse) to build an opponent-adjusted scoring model. The model ranks every player on my roster and every available free agent at each position, then recommends the starting lineup, waiver swaps, and realistic trades that maximize my expected points — bench-only and never-lose-value are hard rules for trade suggestions, not soft preferences. Self-hosted on the same Raspberry Pi as the weather station and golf advisor.

Russian Losses vs. Propaganda Model

Tests whether the volume and tone of Russian state-media war coverage has explanatory power over Russia's monthly personnel losses in Ukraine. Pulls war-coverage volume/tone from major Moscow outlets (GDELT), Vladimir Putin's public-appearance schedule (Kremlin.ru, via Wayback Machine archives, since the live site blocks this network), and self-scored sentiment on posts from several widely-cited Russian war-correspondent Telegram channels. Each month is estimated by a regression trained only on prior months, then refit once that month's actual figures are in — the explanatory variables (capped at 4) are chosen automatically by genuine out-of-sample walk-forward accuracy across the whole series, not by hindsight fit. Self-hosted on the same Raspberry Pi as the weather station and fantasy football optimizer.
